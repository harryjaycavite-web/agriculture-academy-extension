Extension Principles & History

📜 Foundations of Agricultural Extension

  • Philosophy of Extension — working WITH people, not FOR them; education-based, voluntary, grassroots-oriented.
  • Historical Evolution — from early demonstration farms to the formal Extension Service under the Department of Agriculture.
  • Devolution (RA 7160) — the 1991 Local Government Code transferred agricultural extension delivery to LGUs.

Extension Methods & Strategies

🧑‍🌾 Extension Teaching Methods

  • Individual Methods — farm/home visits, personal consultations; high cost per farmer, high impact.
  • Group Methods — demonstrations, field days, farmer field schools; balanced cost and reach.
  • Mass Methods — radio, print, social media; lowest cost per farmer, widest reach.

Technology Adoption & Diffusion

📈 Diffusion of Innovations

  • Adopter Categories — Innovators → Early Adopters → Early Majority → Late Majority → Laggards.
  • Adoption S-Curve — cumulative adoption starts slow, accelerates, then plateaus over time.
  • Innovation-Decision Process — Knowledge → Persuasion → Decision → Implementation → Confirmation.
